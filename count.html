The AP and DDHQ links open each outlet's live county data feed. Neither has changed a county's Senate total since Nov. 6 (evening, CT).

Each outlet is ahead of the other somewhere. DDHQ has newer batches in four counties (Lowndes, Chickasaw, Tippah, George). AP has a newer batch in one: Holmes County, where AP added C 84, H 8 on Nov. 6 that DDHQ never picked up (AP Holmes 5,542 / 841 v DDHQ 5,458 / 833). Neither carries Washington County's own published results. So DDHQ plus Washington is not this count: DDHQ is still missing the Holmes batch.

Not yet in any count

Affidavit ballots still under review and mail ballots that arrive by the Nov. 10 deadline. The Secretary of State reports 12,767 affidavits processed statewide at about 77% accepted and just over 2,800 mail ballots outstanding (Watson, Nov. 8). On Nov. 9 he estimated about 8,000 affidavits still to process (SuperTalk Mississippi). The largest pending counties have said how many they received: Hinds, DeSoto (about 2,100), Rankin (1,667), Harrison (1,448), Jackson (1,400+). Counties certify by Nov. 13.
